How to play
Letters and numbers fall down the panel, one or two at a time. Press the matching key on your keyboard; when two fall together, hold both down at the same time to clear them, before they reach the bottom. Pressing a key that isn't on screen, or letting a key reach the bottom, fails the hack. There are 2 phases of 10 keys; the red bar shows your progress. Keys: Enter = start, Esc = restart.
