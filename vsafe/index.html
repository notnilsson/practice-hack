How to play
Click two squares to flip them. If the icons match they stay red; if not, they flash and flip back. Match all 8 pairs to clear a phase. There are 2 phases and no way to fail – it only counts your time and misses. The red bar is the phase timer from the clip (about 40 seconds). Keys: R = restart, Enter = start.
