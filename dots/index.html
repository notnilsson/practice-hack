How to play
You start on a random dot (already lit). Click the dot that is closest to the last dot you connected, then the closest to that one, and so on until every dot is connected. Only dots you haven't connected yet count. One wrong click fails the hack. There are 2 phases of 10 dots, each with its own timer (the red bar, about 11 seconds). Keys: R = restart, Enter = start.
