How to play
Step 1 – terminal: type each [TASK] line exactly and press Enter (3 attempts; type help to see the tasks again).
Step 2 – rings: rebuild the captured signature from the inside out. For each of the 4 rings, cycle the fragments with the ←/→ arrow keys (or A/D, the ◀▶ buttons or the thumbnails) and press Enter when the fragment matches that ring on the left. Decoys differ by a small detail — an arc gap, a tick, a yellow square or a marker. Compare one feature at a time. R = restart (outside the terminal).
