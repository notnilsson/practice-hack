Practice settings & rules
How to play: balls drop toward the gate (the two red balls at the bottom). Hold left click (anywhere on the board) or hold Space to open the gate and let orange balls through; let go to close it so red balls are blocked. Each correct ball fills the bar; fill it to clear the phase. There are 3 phases and the speed stays the same. Like in-game, letting a red ball in or blocking an orange one fails the hack instantly (turn that off below to practise with a progress penalty instead). The gate takes ~0.2s to open, so start holding just before an orange ball arrives. Keys: R = restart, Enter = start.
