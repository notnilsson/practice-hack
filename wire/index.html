Fleeca – Wire Cut
7 wires show their cut order for 3 seconds. Then the numbers vanish and the wires cross over.
Cut them in order 1 → 7 within 10 seconds. One wrong cut fails.
How to play
Seven coloured wires each show a number from 1–7 for about 3 seconds (the short bar under "Memorise the cut order"). Then the numbers disappear and the right-hand ends swap places, so the wires cross. The colours and the left-hand ends stay where they were. Click the wires in order from 1 to 7 to cut them. One wrong cut fails, and so does running out of the 10 second timer. There is only 1 phase. Keys: R = restart, Enter = start.
