How to play
How to play: during Watch the pattern the orange squares keep jumping around — only the final arrangement counts. It freezes and glows yellow (Memorize the squares), fades out, then you left-click every square that was lit. Matches the clip: 7×7 grid, 7 squares, ~2.4s shuffle, ~1.1s to memorize, 35s to select. Tip: squares that stop moving early are usually part of the final pattern. Keys: R = restart, Enter = start.
