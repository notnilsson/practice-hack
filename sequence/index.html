How to play
How to solve: enter the 4 codes shown at the top, in order. Pick 1 → 2 in the same column, 2 → 3 in the same row, 3 → 4 in the same column. The picks don't need to be next to each other. Any wrong click fails the stage. Hovering a cell lights up (yellow) the line your next pick must come from. Keys: R = restart, Enter = start.
