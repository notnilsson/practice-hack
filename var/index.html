How to play
Eight squares numbered 1–8 appear for about 3 seconds (the short red bar under "Memorise the order") while they slowly drift. Then the numbers go blank and the squares keep moving. Click them in order from 1 to 8 – the big red number shows which one to find next. A correct square turns green and disappears. One wrong click fails, and so does running out of the 20 second timer. There is only 1 phase. Keys: R = restart, Enter = start.
